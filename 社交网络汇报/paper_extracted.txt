            Automated schedule generation for multitask

            ing on solver-specific code generation. This
            raises critical questions: Can large language
            models (LLMs) solve this NL ⇒ Schedule task
            end-to-end well (RQ1)? If the answer is "no",
            where do they fall short (RQ2)? And how
            can their capabilities be enhanced (RQ3)? To
            answer these questions, we introduce NL ⇒
